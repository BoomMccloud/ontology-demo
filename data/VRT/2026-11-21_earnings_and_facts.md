## 2. Organic Orders, Backlog & Guidance

* **Organic Orders:** Surged **>100% YoY** (more than doubled) in Q3 2026, which management attributed to genuine acceleration in AI infrastructure deployments rather than pull-forward or catch-up ordering (CEO Giordano Albertazzi / Executive Chairman David Cote, Q3 2026 earnings call, 2026-10-28).
* **Backlog & Reporting Policy:** Vertiv stopped disclosing explicit quarterly dollar backlog and exact book-to-bill ratios earlier in 2026. However, management stated that reported backlog reflects **firm purchase orders** (excluding speculative capacity reservations) and supports **12–18 months** of delivery visibility extending well into 2027 and beyond.

## 3. Liquid Cooling, Power Infrastructure & M&A Execution

* **800 VDC Power Architecture (November 2026):** Building on the earlier 2026 launch of its *PowerDirect 5000 Power Rack*, Vertiv expanded its **800 VDC** portfolio across rack, compute pod, and data hall deployment levels (PR Newswire / Vertiv, Nov 2026). The architecture streamlines conversion from medium-voltage grids to compute trays, cutting conversion losses and floor space for multi-megawatt AI clusters.
* **NVIDIA (`NVDA`) Joint Engineering — ATLAS Lab & Vera Rubin:**
  * In **November 2026**, Vertiv unveiled the **ATLAS Lab** (*Advanced Test Lab for Architecture and Systems*) developed with `NVDA` to stress-test power, liquid cooling, and controls under simulated AI workloads ahead of physical silicon deployment using *NVIDIA Omniverse* and *DSX AI Factory* digital twin libraries.
  * Vertiv delivers *DSX SimReady* power/cooling models, **Vertiv™ OneCore** modular building blocks, and *SmartIT* pre-engineered OCP/MGX pods supporting `NVDA` Blackwell (`GB200`/`GB300`) and next-generation **Vera Rubin** (`NVL72` / 2,000W+ per-chip) racks.
* **Microsoft (`MSFT`) & Hyperscale Liquid Cooling:** Direct-to-chip (cold plate) cooling accounts for ~80%+ of AI liquid cooling deployments alongside `Vertiv Liebert` thermal lines and `XDU` Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs). `MSFT` has scaled liquid-cooled Azure AI superfactories (including Wisconsin and Atlanta) engineered for high-density `NVDA` platforms.
* **Strategic M&A (Grid-to-Fluid Expansion):**
  * **UtilityInnovation Group (UIG):** Announced September 2026 and closed for **~$1.45B** (Vertiv / The Tech Capital), adding microgrid controls, behind-the-meter power architectures, and onsite generation orchestration to mitigate grid-interconnect bottlenecks ("time to power").
  * **King Environmental Services Ltd. (KES):** Ireland-based firm acquired in **October 2026** (Vertiv / Refindustry), extending liquid-cooling fluid management, commissioning, and thermal load-testing into EMEA alongside North American capabilities from the prior **PurgeRite** acquisition.
