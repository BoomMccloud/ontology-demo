## 3. Silicon, Packaging & Supplier Relationships

- **Edge AI & On-Device Silicon — A20 Pro (TSMC 2nm):**
  - Powering iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max and iPhone Duo, the **A20 Pro** is Apple's first smartphone SoC built on **TSMC's 2nm (N2)** GAA process, delivering higher transistor density and Neural Engine efficiency for local AI inference.
- **Advanced Semiconductor Packaging — TSMC WMCM:**
  - Transitioned from legacy Package-on-Package (PoP) stacking to **WMCM (Wafer-level Multi-Chip Module)** side-by-side logic and DRAM placement, expanding memory bandwidth and improving thermal dissipation to prevent throttling during sustained on-device AI workloads (Impress, BigGo, Crypto Briefing).
- **Custom Silicon & Modem Independence — C2 Modem (vs. QCOM):**
  - Paired with A20 Pro, Apple's in-house **C2 cellular modem** adds native U.S. mmWave support and ~15% lower power consumption, further reducing reliance on **Qualcomm (QCOM)** (Apple, CNET).
- **Memory & Foundry Dependencies:**
  - Advanced Edge AI features enforce strict RAM floors (12GB+ unified memory on flagship devices), increasing Apple's sensitivity to DRAM/NAND price inflation (explicitly cited in Q1 FY27 margin guidance).
  - Apple remains **TSMC's** anchor leading-edge customer while diversifying U.S. fab exposure via **TSMC Arizona** to mitigate geopolitical and tariff risks.
