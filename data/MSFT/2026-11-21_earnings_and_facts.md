## 1. Recent Earnings Highlights

### Q1 FY27 (Quarter Ended 2026-09-30; Reported 2026-10-28)
*Sources: Microsoft Q1 FY27 Earnings Release, Quartr, Vested Finance, DataStudios*

- **Headline Results:**
  - **Total Revenue:** \$92.3B (+19% YoY), beating consensus expectations.
  - **Operating Income:** \$45.9B (+21% YoY).
  - **Net Income & EPS:** GAAP net income \$36.6B (+32% YoY) / GAAP diluted EPS \$4.92 (+32% YoY); Non-GAAP net income (excluding OpenAI investment impacts) \$36.4B (+18% YoY) / Non-GAAP diluted EPS \$4.89 (+18% YoY).
  - **Operating Cash Flow & FCF:** Operating cash flow grew 29% YoY to \$58.0B; cash paid for property and equipment totaled \$39.4B, bringing quarterly **Free Cash Flow (FCF)** to **\$18.6B** (down ~27% YoY due to upfront infrastructure outlays).
  - **Quarterly Dividend:** Raised to \$0.98 per share.
- **Segment Performance (Post-Reorganization):**
  - *Note:* Via an **8-K filing on 2026-09-02**, Microsoft retired its legacy three-segment reporting (*Productivity and Business Processes*, *Intelligent Cloud*, *More Personal Computing*) and consolidated operations into two segments:
  1. **Agents and Infra:** \$76.8B revenue (+25% YoY).
     - **Microsoft Cloud:** \$63.9B (+30% YoY).
     - **Azure & Other Cloud Services:** Grew **48% YoY** to **\$33.2B** (accelerating from 43% in Q4 FY26), fueled by enterprise AI workloads and cloud consumption.
     - **Microsoft 365 Commercial Cloud:** +18% YoY, supported by M365 Copilot, E5, and early E7 tier upgrades.
     - **Commercial RPO (Backlog):** Surged 73% YoY to **\$677B**.
  2. **Devices and Consumer:** \$15.5B revenue (-3% YoY).
     - Windows OEM & devices declined 22% YoY; Xbox content & services declined 2% YoY; Search & advertising (ex-TAC) grew 12% YoY.
- **Q2 FY27 Guidance (Provided 2026-10-28):**
  - **Total Revenue:** \$96.0B–\$97.1B.
  - **Azure Growth:** Guided to **49%–50% YoY** in constant currency, with management reiterating that AI demand continues to exceed available supply.
  - **M365 Commercial Cloud:** Projected ~19% constant-currency growth.

### Q4 FY26 (Quarter Ended 2026-06-30; Reported 2026-07-29)
*Sources: Microsoft Q4 FY26 Release, MLQ.ai, Directions on Microsoft*

- **Total Revenue:** \$90.0B (+18% YoY); **Operating Income:** \$40.6B (+18% YoY).
- **GAAP Net Income / EPS:** \$35.8B / \$4.81 (Non-GAAP: \$35.3B / \$4.74).
- **Cloud & Azure:** Microsoft Cloud reached \$59.3B; Azure grew **43% YoY**, pushing **full-year FY26 Azure revenue past the \$100B milestone**.
- **Capex:** Cash capex was ~\$41B (\$35.8B cash P&E additions); full-year FY26 capex reached **\$115.9B**.

## 2. AI Capex & Data Center Buildout Facts

- **Quarterly & Annual Capex Trajectory:**
  - Q1 FY27 total capital expenditures and finance leases reached **\$45.2B** (~30% YoY increase; ~75% directed to short-lived AI compute assets, servers, and data center equipment), with \$39.4B in cash P&E.
  - On the **2026-10-28** earnings call (reported by *Business Insider* and *Fortune*), CFO Amy Hood slightly trimmed **calendar-year 2026 Capex guidance from \$175B to ~\$170B**. Management stressed the \$5B trim reflected third-party delivery slips (specifically "GPU-as-a-Service" contracts and power/data center construction timing), not softer demand, with quarterly capex projected to top **\$50B** as delayed capacity lands in 2H FY27.
- **Flagship "Fairwater" AI Superfactories:**
  - Microsoft is deploying planet-scale AI training clusters under its **Fairwater** initiative (key sites in **Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin** and **Atlanta, Georgia**), linked via a dedicated **AI WAN**.
  - These liquid-cooled facilities pack hundreds of thousands of **NVIDIA (`NVDA`) Blackwell (GB200 NVL72)** GPUs operating at **120–140+ kW per rack**, far exceeding legacy air-cooled enterprise data center limits.
- **Custom Silicon & Model Stack (Microsoft Ignite, 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-20):**
  - **Maia 300 AI Accelerator:** Unveiled at Ignite 2026, engineered to deliver ~40% more tokens per watt vs. traditional GPU systems to lower internal inference costs and reduce long-term reliance on `NVDA`.
  - **Cobalt 200 Arm VMs:** Generally available, offering up to 50% higher performance over Cobalt 100 for general-purpose cloud workloads.
  - **Proprietary MAI & Partner Models:** Microsoft introduced **MAI Code 2 / Code 2 Flash** and **Microsoft-Decision-1.1** alongside OpenAI's **GPT-6.2 (Astra/Sol)** series and Anthropic models in **Microsoft Foundry**, routing 30%–40% of simpler queries to smaller in-house models to protect gross margins.

## 3. Key Ecosystem & Peer Relationships

- **Compute & Thermal Suppliers:** **NVIDIA (`NVDA`)** (primary GPU/system supplier; collaborating on 800 VDC facility power architectures and Vera Rubin DSX blueprints); **Vertiv (`VRT`)** (critical liquid-cooling, heat-exchanger, and pre-engineered power-block partner operating the `NVDA`-linked ATLAS Lab); **CoreWeave** and **Nebius** (neocloud GPU-as-a-Service partners, including Nebius's Vineland, NJ contract, though Microsoft tightened oversight and recalibrated delayed third-party capacity in late 2026).
- **Energy & Grid Partners:** **Constellation Energy (`CEG`)** (long-term nuclear power purchase agreement centered on the Crane Clean Energy Center / Three Mile Island restart to secure firm carbon-free baseload power).
- **AI Lab Partners:** **OpenAI** (anchor Azure consumer, Frontier model partner, and major driver of Microsoft's \$677B RPO, though also a source of non-operating equity-method loss exposure); **Anthropic** (hosted in Microsoft Foundry / Copilot multi-model routing).
- **Peers & Competitors:** **Amazon (`AMZN` / AWS)** and **Alphabet (`GOOGL` / GCP)** (hyperscale cloud and enterprise AI rivals); **Oracle (`ORCL`)** (competing/overlapping on OpenAI and GPU cloud capacity); **Apple (`AAPL`)** (pursues a contrasting hybrid/custom-M-series Private Cloud Compute model rather than matching hyperscaler/neocloud capex intensity).
