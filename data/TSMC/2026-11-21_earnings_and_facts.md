# TSMC (NYSE: TSM / TWSE: 2330) — Earnings, Capacity & Operational Facts

**As of:** 2026-11-21  
**Primary Sources:** TSMC Q3 2026 Earnings Release & Call (2026-10-15, SEC / TSMC IR), TSMC October 2026 Monthly Revenue Report (2026-11-10, Focus Taiwan / Digitimes), TrendForce, Tom's Hardware

---

## 1. Investment Themes Anchored by Operations
- **AI Infrastructure & Capex:** Hyperscaler and accelerator silicon demand pushed High-Performance Computing (HPC) to **68%** of Q3 2026 revenue, prompting TSMC to maintain a record **$60B–$64B** 2026 capex budget.
- **Advanced Semiconductor Packaging:** CoWoS remains the primary physical bottleneck in AI hardware scaling, driving rapid conversions (AP8) and greenfield mega-sites (AP7) alongside OSAT overflow to **Amkor (AMKR)** and **ASE/SPIL**.
- **Edge AI:** Smartphone revenue accounted for **21%** of Q3 2026 revenue (+10% QoQ), anchored by **Apple (AAPL)**, **Qualcomm (QCOM)**, and **MediaTek** ramping leading-edge N3/N2 silicon for on-device AI workloads.
- **Supply Chain Localization:** Multi-billion-dollar fab commitments across **Arizona (Fab 21)**, **Japan (JASM)**, and **Germany (ESMC)** hedge geopolitical concentration risk while introducing 2–4 percentage points of near-term gross margin dilution.

  - **Operating Margin:** **58.7%** | **Net Profit Margin:** **51.1%**.
- **Revenue by Technology Node:** Advanced nodes (7nm and below) reached **79%** of total wafer revenue:

  - **N3 (3nm):** **33%**
  - **N5 (5nm):** **28%**
  - **N7 (7nm):** **10%**
- **Revenue by Platform:**
  - **HPC:** **68%** (+22% QoQ)
  - **Smartphone:** **21%** (+10% QoQ)
  - **Automotive:** **4%** | **IoT:** **4%** | **DCE:** **1%** | **Others:** **2%**

### Q4 2026 Guidance & October 2026 Monthly Print
- **Q4 2026 Revenue Guidance:** **US$52.3B–$54.3B** (~58% YoY at midpoint); **Gross Margin:** **65%–67%**; **Operating Margin:** **56.5%–58.5%**.

- **Shareholder Return:** Trailing dividend yield ~**1.2%–1.3%** as of late November 2026.

## 3. Leading-Edge Nodes & CoWoS Packaging Capacity
- **N2 (2nm GAA) Ramp & A16 Roadmap:**
  - Commercial production ramping across **Fab 20 (Hsinchu)** and **Fab 22 (Kaohsiung)**; already **8%** of Q3 2026 wafer revenue.
  - Management expects N2 family capacity to **more than double in 2027** (industry trackers project ~90k–120k wafers per month [wpm] across phases into 2027).
  - **A16 (1.6nm)** with backside power delivery follows N2; industry estimates place capital intensity for N2/A16 at roughly **US$28B–$30B per 50k wpm**.
  - Key supplier dependency: **ASML** EUV lithography systems remain critical to N2/N3 scaling.
- **CoWoS (Advanced Packaging) Scaling:**
  - Projected exit capacity: **~127k–130k wpm by end-2026**, scaling toward **200k–260k wpm across 2027–2028** (80%+ CAGR from 2022–2027).
  - Ultra-large reticle CoWoS lines supporting accelerators such as **NVIDIA (NVDA)** Blackwell and Rubin architectures reportedly achieve **>98% yields** (Design-Reuse / industry reports).
  - **Domestic Packaging Hubs:** **AP8 (Tainan)**—converted from the acquired Innolux LCD plant (~96k sq m)—is providing critical near-term 2026 volume, while **AP7 (Chiayi)** serves as a long-term CoWoS and **SoIC** mega-campus.
  - Despite rapid expansion, industry trackers (TrendForce) expect a **10%–30% supply-demand gap** to persist, spilling overflow demand to **Amkor (AMKR)** and **ASE/SPIL**.
